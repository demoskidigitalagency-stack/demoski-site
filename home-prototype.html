Your first 30 days

What happens after you say yes.

  1. Day 0

    Strategy call

    Free, 30 minutes. We look at your offer, your numbers and your market, and tell you what we would and would not do.

  2. Days 1–5

    Setup

    Ad accounts structured, tracking installed and tested, first creative produced.

  3. Launch day

    Go live

    Campaigns start. Paid ads can bring the first leads the same day.

  4. Day 2–3

    First real data

    Within 24–48 hours there is enough to read. We cut what is weak and feed what works.

  5. Day 30

    Report and next step

    Performance report and a call. You decide whether to scale, hold or stop.

Before you ask

Straight answers.

What is the smallest budget you work with?

₦100,000 a month in ad spend, or the equivalent in dollars. Below that there is not enough data to optimise.

Is ad spend part of your fee?

No. You pay Meta, Google or TikTok directly for ads. Our fee covers strategy, setup, creative, management and reporting.

Can I take one service only?

Yes. Take one layer or the whole system. Larger projects can be paid 50% up front and 50% on delivery.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across Africa, the UK, the US and Canada.

How fast do you reply?

Within one hour during business hours, on WhatsApp or email.
